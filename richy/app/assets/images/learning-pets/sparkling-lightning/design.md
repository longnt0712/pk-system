# Tia sét lấp lánh

The main bolt, small companion bolt, and inner highlight reuse the exact paths from `../../battle-lightning.svg`, the lightning used in online Lụm Trứng. Its yellow-to-orange gradient and dark outline are preserved. A soft warm halo and a few small diamond glints distinguish this level-20 collectible at both small and large display sizes.

`pet-level-20.svg` is the editable vector source. `pet-level-20.png` is a 1024 × 1024 RGBA render with transparent background. The pet is fully formed when unlocked at level 20; there are no egg or cracked-egg stages. Runtime sparkle animation is applied by the pet UI.
