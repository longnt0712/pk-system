# Bí ngô Halloween

An original, editable vector jack-o-lantern with rounded orange ribs, a curled green stem, a small leaf, and friendly warm-yellow carved features. `pet.svg` is the source; `pet.png` is its transparent 1024 × 1024 RGBA render. The pet is available fully formed at every vocabulary level, including level 0; there are no egg stages.

The seasonal selection window runs every year from October 1 through November 5 inclusive, using UTC+7 (Asia/Bangkok). Administrators may choose it all year. The application enforces availability separately from these assets. This folder intentionally has no `pet.json`, so the asset does not create an extra Lụm Trứng prize or change its probabilities.
